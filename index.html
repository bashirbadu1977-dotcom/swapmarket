SwapMarket

Buy. Sell. Negotiate. Trade.

A simple marketplace where people can sell items, make offers, negotiate prices, or propose a trade.

How SwapMarket can earn money: take a small fee when a sale is completed, charge for promoted listings, and offer optional seller plans. Never promise sellers a guaranteed profit.
Example: if a seller completes a $100 sale and your marketplace fee is 4%, your platform earns $4 before payment-processing and other costs.
